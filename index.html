How to Play

Players

Two players — X and O. X always goes first.

Core Rules

  1. Played on a 3×3 grid.
  2. Take turns clicking an empty cell.
  3. You can't overwrite a taken cell.
  4. Game ends on a win or full board.

How to Win

Get 3 marks in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally.

Draw

All 9 cells filled with no winner? It's a draw.

Restart

Hit Restart to reset the board and play again.
